Moist Banana Cake

A simple, delicious way to use ripe bananas for a perfect afternoon snack.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas (mashed)
  • 3 eggs
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1.5 cups sugar
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 180°C and grease a baking pan.
  2. In a blender, mix the eggs, oil, and mashed bananas until smooth.
  3. In a large bowl, sift together the flour, sugar, and cinnamon.
  4. Pour the liquid mixture into the dry ingredients and stir until well combined.
  5. Gently fold in the baking powder.
  6. Pour the batter into the pan and bake for 35–40 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean.
