Traditional Bolinho de Chuva

These "Raindrop Fritters" are a classic Brazilian comfort food, perfect with a cup of coffee.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2.5 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • Oil (for frying)
  • Cinnamon and sugar (for coating)

Instructions

  1. In a medium bowl, whisk the eggs and sugar until pale.
  2. Gradually add the milk and flour, mixing until you have a thick, smooth batter.
  3. Stir in the baking powder at the end.
  4. Heat the oil in a deep pan. Use two spoons to drop small portions of dough into the hot oil.
  5. Fry until golden brown on all sides.
  6. Drain on paper towels and immediately roll them in the cinnamon-sugar mixture while still warm.
